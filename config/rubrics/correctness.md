Correctness: every statement agrees with the provided facts and policy decisions. No invented dates, amounts, statuses, tracking numbers or promises (delivery dates, refund dates, compensation, exceptions). Customer-quoted facts that are wrong must not be repeated as true. Claims that something was done (refund issued, return created, order cancelled) must match the recorded actions.
5 = fully correct. 4 = correct, one soft statement not backed by facts (e.g. generic "banks take a few days"). 3 = one minor inaccuracy or unsupported promise. 2 = a clear factual error. 1 = several errors, or a false claim that an action happened.
